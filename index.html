About Us

Why Life Flow Matters

Life Flow is a community blood donation initiative by 98 Friends — passionate volunteers from Sooriyawewa dedicated to saving lives.

🎯

Our Mission

Build a sustainable blood donation ecosystem in Sooriyawewa — ensuring no patient ever suffers from blood shortages.

🤝

98 Friends Community

98 passionate friends united by one belief: every drop of donated blood gives another life a second chance.

📍

Location

Sooriyawewa, Southern Province, Sri Lanka. Serving local hospitals and health centers with regular drives.
